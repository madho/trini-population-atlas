Method note
What is measured?
The source is Census table B04006, “People Reporting Ancestry.” Percentages are calculated as the Trinidadian/Tobagonian estimate divided by the table’s total-population estimate. ACS values are estimates with sampling uncertainty; MOE is shown where available.
Source: U.S. Census Bureau · ACS 2020–2024 5-Year Estimates · Table B04006Built for exploration, not exact enumeration.
